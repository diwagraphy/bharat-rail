    Driving

    Drag the master controller right to add power, one notch at a time. Pull the A9 handle down to brake, and ease it back up just before you stop. Sudden changes spill chai in the coaches.
    Sound the horn at every yellow W/L board. After a yellow signal, stay under 50 km/h until the next one. Stop before red.
    On wet rails, add power slowly or press Sand. Cruise holds line speed and hands control back 2 km before a halt.
    Keys: →← notch · ↓↑ A9 · [] dyn brake · H horn · X sand · C cruise · E emergency · Esc pause
